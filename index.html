4. Capacity calculation

Transparent rule: the tracker first calculates a 0–100 readiness score, maps it to the nearest half-step (1–5), and then applies a hard duty cap. A 13 h duty today caps capacity at 2.0; a 24/26 h duty today caps it at 1.0. Previous-day duty also imposes a recovery cap. High self-ratings cannot override a heavy duty day.
